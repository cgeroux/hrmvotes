• Minutes search:
    • Empty search text matches all minutes.
    • Can use "and" and "or" to combine search terms, along with brackets. For example the search "(bike or bus) and lane" will find all the motions that contains the words bike and lane or bus and lane.
  • Selecting motions:
    • Once you have performed a search of meeting minutes and the results are displayed, you can select different motions by clicking the box to the left of a given motion to a maximum of 20 selected motions.
  • Displaying motion votes:
    • Once you have at least one motion selected, you can display it by clicking the "Display Votes" button.
  • Removing selected motions:
    • Once you have one or more motions selected, you can view them by clicking on the "View Selected Motions" button. There you can check/uncheck motions to update your selection of motions.
  • URL query string:
    • When you click the "Display Votes" button, the URL you are visiting will be updated to include your selected motions. This URL can then be shared to show your selected motions and their votes with others.
  • Displaying Help:
    • Since you are reading this, you likely already know that the "?" button displays this help text. However, clicking it again will hide this help text again to get it out of your way.
